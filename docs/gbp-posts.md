# Google Business Profile posts

A queue to work through, one a week. Posts go stale after about seven days
— an unposted week is a dead profile, and the activity signal is most of
why this is worth doing at all.

## How to post

Profile manager → Promote → Add update. Options are **Update**, **Offer**
and **Event**. Almost everything below is an Update.

**Only the first ~80 characters show** before the fold, so the hook goes
first. Everything after it is for the person who already decided to tap.

**One photo per post.** Reuse from `images/gbp/` or shoot something. A post
with no image gets a fraction of the impressions.

**Pick a button.** "Book" points at the Calendar link in `src/content/site.ts`;
"Learn more" points at the relevant page; "Call now" uses the profile number.

**Never invent a fact.** No "20 happy clients", no made-up deadlines on
offers. One real client, real prices, real promises — there is enough.

## What not to post

**Offers with fake deadlines.** "This month only" on a price that is not
changing is a lie a returning visitor can check.

**Anything seasonal you will not take down.** A Christmas post still sitting
there in February says nobody is minding the profile.

**Volume claims.** No "trusted by local businesses" plural until it is
plural and you could name them.
